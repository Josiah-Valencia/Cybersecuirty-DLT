Three ways in

Find where you fit

These roles cover the full arc of defense — breaking in on purpose, watching in real time, and reconstructing what happened after the fact.

Role 01

Ethical Hacker

  • Tests systems and networks for security weaknesses.
  • Identifies vulnerabilities before malicious attackers can exploit them.
  • Helps organizations strengthen their security posture.
Role 02

Security Operations Center Analyst

  • Monitors systems and networks for suspicious activity.
  • Detects and responds to cybersecurity threats and incidents.
  • Helps protect an organization's systems and data.
Role 03

Forensic Investigator

  • Investigates cybersecurity incidents and attacks.
  • Collects and analyzes digital evidence.
  • Helps determine what happened and how the incident occurred.
